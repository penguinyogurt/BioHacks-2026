**What the scene needs to handle:**
- **Thin 3D projection.** The first three axes carry only 10.05% of the variance (5.9 / 2.1 / 2.0%), so the screen has to say that distances come from the full 400-d vectors.
- **Long names.** Some match names are long IUPAC strings, so the scene must truncate them.
- **Unnamed matches.** Three matches have no name source at all (DB14751, DB05744, DB05210 in the summary; DB05241 also prints as a bare ID). Those labels fall back to the bare DrugBank ID.
- **Sparse disease links.** Only C3, TARDBP, SLC1A2, GRIA2 and NFE2L2 have a direct edge to the ALS node (`alsEdge`); the others are null.

**Where I deviated:**
1. **Extra name source.** I added Wikidata's DrugBank-ID property (P715), cached in the gitignored `data/raw/drkg/wikidata-drugbank-names.tsv`. 2. **More name sources.** Levi's `ingredient_names` from `agentic-query-generation` is also used, and compound labels get their first letter capitalised.
3. **Hover names trimmed.** `names.json` leaves out Gene Ontology terms (biological process, molecular function, cellular component) to stay under 1 MB.
4. **Edge count.** `targetEdges` counts every DRKG triple between a compound and that gene, across all sources, not unique compounds.
