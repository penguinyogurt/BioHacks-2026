FaceCheck

Record your relaxed face and five expressions. On a later check-in, compare your facial movement with your own baseline.

Take your time getting into each expression — nothing advances on a timer, you click when you're ready. Go as big and exaggerated as you can for each one; a subtle expression won't read as different from a weak one.
