## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. The clinical effect depends on the study population. Mouse ALS experiments show drug-specific increased cortical exposure with FUS, but do not demonstrate a human ALS treatment was exposure-limited. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28522181/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25286015/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36931463/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. Mouse data support enhanced cortical delivery of edaravone. Translation to safe, effective human CNS exposure remains unresolved. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36931463/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:28522181 · favorable

Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled phase III. Population: Selected early-stage ALS population. Intervention: IV infusion.

Primary endpoint: ALSFRS-R change at 24 weeks.

Observed outcome: Decline was smaller in the selected early-stage cohort.

Safety: Treatment-emergent events occurred in 84% of each arm.

The selected population limits generalization.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28522181/)

### PMID:25286015 · negative

Double-blind parallel placebo-controlled trial. Population: Broader ALS population than later positive trial. Intervention: IV infusion.

Primary endpoint: 24-week ALSFRS-R change.

Observed outcome: The between-arm difference was 0.65 points, p=0.411. The later positive study selected a narrower cohort.

Safety: Safety was assessed; the comparison informs the scope of efficacy.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25286015/)
