# Trehalose

**Disposition: negative efficacy; retained only.**

Human evidence category: negative efficacy; retained only.

No evidence of slower disease progression. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40409314/)

## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. IV dosing bypassed intestinal degradation. The HEALEY and expanded-access reports do not identify an exposure-limited ALS response. A FUS nanoparticle experiment used trehalose in the formulation and measured delivery of another cargo. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40409314/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40857020/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40605559/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:40409314 · negative

Randomized placebo-controlled HEALEY phase II/III regimen. Population: HEALEY ALS platform participants. Intervention: Weekly intravenous trehalose.

Primary endpoint: Joint rate of ALSFRS-R progression and survival over 24 weeks.

Observed outcome: No evidence of slower disease progression.

Safety: Serious adverse events were more frequent in the active arm. Investigators attributed none of seven active-arm deaths to trehalose.

No supportive secondary biomarker or clinical effect.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40409314/)

### PMID:40857020 · safety

Single-arm expanded-access protocol. Population: ALS patients in expanded access, including prior HEALEY participants. Intervention: Weekly IV trehalose.

Primary endpoint: Safety and biological activity; function tracked.

Observed outcome: Function declined over follow-up. No concurrent control established efficacy.

Safety: Serious adverse events and deaths occurred; investigators reported no unexpected treatment-related risk.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40857020/)
