# Levetiracetam

**Disposition: unresolved.**

Human evidence category: unresolved.

Cramp and phasic spasticity measures improved without a concurrent placebo group. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18821142/)

## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. Uncontrolled symptomatic improvement does not establish efficacy or an exposure deficit. A controlled cramp outcome is the missing evidence. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18821142/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:18821142 · inconclusive

Unblinded within-person pilot with a three-month baseline. Population: 20 with motor neuron disease including ALS. Intervention: Oral levetiracetam.

Primary endpoint: Changes in cramp severity/frequency and spasticity.

Observed outcome: Cramp and phasic spasticity measures improved without a concurrent placebo group.

Safety: The authors reported good tolerability.

A blinded symptomatic trial would clarify the signal.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18821142/)
