# Gabapentin

**Disposition: excluded by case screen.**

Human evidence category: negative efficacy; retained only.

No strength, survival or functional benefit. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11294919/)

Conservative exclusion for reduced respiratory reserve in this case. Section 5.8 warns of respiratory depression with underlying respiratory impairment; this is a monitoring warning, not an absolute contraindication. [dailymed.nlm.nih.gov](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=1045db4e-4c76-4a0d-bf12-019bd96a8f59)

## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. Phase III failed to confirm the earlier arm-strength trend. No reviewed evidence links that failure to insufficient regional exposure. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11294919/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8960715/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:11294919 · negative

Placebo-controlled randomized phase III. Population: People with ALS. Intervention: Oral gabapentin.

Primary endpoint: Decline in arm muscle strength.

Observed outcome: No strength, survival or functional benefit.

Safety: Combined phase II/III analyses showed faster FVC decline in the gabapentin group.

The pooled respiratory finding is separate from the phase III primary endpoint.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11294919/)

### PMID:8960715 · inconclusive

Randomized placebo-controlled phase II. Population: 152 ALS participants. Intervention: Oral gabapentin 800 mg three times daily.

Primary endpoint: Slope of arm strength decline.

Observed outcome: A favorable trend did not reach significance; phase III did not confirm benefit.

Safety: Generally tolerated during this trial.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8960715/)
