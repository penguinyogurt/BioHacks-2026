# Rasagiline

**Disposition: negative efficacy; retained only.**

Human evidence category: negative efficacy; retained only.

No significant difference in primary survival outcome. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29934198/) No improvement in functional slope or convincing peripheral target engagement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30192007/)

## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. Two studies gave differing peripheral biomarker signals. Neither identifies a CNS concentration shortfall responsible for the neutral survival and function results. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29934198/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25832828/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30192007/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:29934198 · negative

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled phase II. Population: People with ALS on riluzole. Intervention: Oral rasagiline added to riluzole.

Primary endpoint: Survival time.

Observed outcome: No significant difference in primary survival outcome.

Safety: Adverse-event frequencies were comparable; respiratory failure occurred in both arms.

A post hoc faster-progressor signal requires prospective confirmation.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29934198/)

### PMID:25832828 · biomarker

Open-label screening study with historical comparisons. Population: People with ALS. Intervention: Oral rasagiline.

Primary endpoint: ALSFRS-R trajectory and exploratory mitochondrial biomarkers.

Observed outcome: Functional decline did not improve. Mitochondrial and apoptosis markers changed.

Safety: Nausea was reported.

Historical cohorts differed in symptom duration.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25832828/)

### PMID:30192007 · negative

Randomized controlled trial with an augmented historical comparison. Population: People with ALS. Intervention: Oral rasagiline.

Primary endpoint: Average ALSFRS-R decline over 12 months.

Observed outcome: No improvement in functional slope or convincing peripheral target engagement.

Safety: Reported as well tolerated, without serious adverse events.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30192007/)

### PMID:38240416 · exploratory

Secondary analysis of the RAS-ALS trial. Population: Patients in the original randomized trial. Intervention: Oral rasagiline with background riluzole.

Primary endpoint: Exploratory heterogeneity by baseline progression and genotype.

Observed outcome: Post hoc phenotype hypotheses require prospective testing.

Safety: No independent safety cohort.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38240416/)
