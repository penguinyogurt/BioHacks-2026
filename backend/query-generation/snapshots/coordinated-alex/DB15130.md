## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. The early dose-comparison signal did not replicate in EMPOWER. No demonstrated regional exposure threshold explains that result or eliminates the neutropenia risk. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22101764/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24067398/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:24067398 · harm

Double-blind randomized EMPOWER phase III. Population: EMPOWER ALS trial. Intervention: Oral dexpramipexole.

Primary endpoint: Joint function and survival rank at 12 months.

Observed outcome: No prespecified clinical efficacy endpoint improved. Neutropenia occurred in 8% versus 2% (37 versus 8 people).

Safety: The comparative hematologic toxicity is the harm basis; the study described overall tolerability as generally good.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24067398/)

### PMID:22101764 · safety

Two-part, double-blind randomized dose-ranging phase II. Population: ALS patients in the safety and dose-comparison cohorts. Intervention: Oral dexpramipexole.

Primary endpoint: Safety and tolerability.

Observed outcome: An exploratory high- versus low-dose functional/survival rank favored higher dose; the larger phase III trial was negative.

Safety: Reported generally well tolerated in this smaller study.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22101764/)

### NCT01622088 · safety

Single-arm extension of EMPOWER. Population: 616 ALS participants. Intervention: Oral dexpramipexole 300 mg/day.

Primary endpoint: Adverse events, serious events and laboratory monitoring.

Observed outcome: Extension data provide no independent controlled efficacy estimate.

Safety: 152 of 616 had a serious adverse event. No concurrent comparator establishes attribution.


[clinicaltrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01622088)

### NCT00931944 · safety

Open-label safety extension. Population: 74 ALS participants. Intervention: Oral dexpramipexole.

Primary endpoint: Hematology, liver, ECG and vital-sign abnormalities.

Observed outcome: Functional outcomes were secondary and uncontrolled.

Safety: The registry records laboratory and vital-sign abnormalities; comparative risk cannot be estimated.


[clinicaltrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00931944)
