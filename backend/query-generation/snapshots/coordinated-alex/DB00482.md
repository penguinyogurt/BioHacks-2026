# Celecoxib

**Disposition: negative efficacy; retained only.**

Human evidence category: negative efficacy; retained only.

No measured functional or survival benefit. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16802291/)

## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. CSF PGE2 did not fall. It was also not elevated at baseline. This leaves inadequate exposure, target selection and disease biology unresolved. Combination studies do not isolate celecoxib. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16802291/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41837970/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:16802291 · negative

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled clinical trial. Population: 300 people with ALS. Intervention: Oral celecoxib 800 mg/day.

Primary endpoint: Rate of decline of maximal voluntary isometric arm strength.

Observed outcome: No measured functional or survival benefit.

Safety: Tolerated at the studied dose without increased overall adverse events.

CSF PGE2 was not elevated at baseline and did not fall on treatment.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16802291/)

### PMID:18608093 · combination

Randomized active-combination selection design. Population: ALS participants in a phase II selection trial. Intervention: Oral celecoxib/creatine versus minocycline/creatine.

Primary endpoint: Six-month ALSFRS-R decline used to select a combination.

Observed outcome: Celecoxib/creatine was selected over minocycline/creatine; effects of individual ingredients could not be isolated.

Safety: Rash was more frequent with celecoxib/creatine.

A historical comparison did not establish placebo-controlled benefit.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18608093/)

### PMID:36106817 · combination

Open-label PrimeC pilot. Population: ALS participants receiving the combination. Intervention: Oral ciprofloxacin/celecoxib fixed combination.

Primary endpoint: Safety and tolerability.

Observed outcome: Exploratory changes cannot isolate a celecoxib effect.

Safety: The combination was generally tolerated in this pilot.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36106817/)

### PMID:41837970 · combination

Blinded randomized PARADIGM phase IIb with open-label extension. Population: Adults with ALS. Intervention: Oral PrimeC (ciprofloxacin plus celecoxib).

Primary endpoint: Safety and tolerability; prespecified biomarker endpoints.

Observed outcome: Signals during follow-up favor further study of the combination. The trial did not demonstrate primary clinical efficacy of celecoxib alone.

Safety: The authors reported tolerability; Alex's riluzole screen excludes ciprofloxacin.

Exploratory clinical outcomes and biomarkers remain combination-specific.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41837970/)
