## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. Controlled trials at different oral doses showed no functional benefit. A small riluzole add-on study measured CSF tau, not a therapeutic receptor-occupancy deficit. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39511965/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20565333/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20839903/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:39511965 · negative

Multicenter double-blind randomized phase IIb. Population: 89 randomized adults with ALS. Intervention: Oral memantine up to 20 mg twice daily.

Primary endpoint: ALSFRS-R progression over 40 weeks.

Observed outcome: No significant difference in progression.

Safety: 52 completed treatment. Attrition limits interpretation; the abstract does not attribute all discontinuations to toxicity.

Neurofilament and neuropsychiatric outcomes supplied no efficacy signal.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39511965/)

### PMID:39307154 · negative

Double-blind randomized MND-SMART platform, stage-two analysis. Population: Motor neuron disease including ALS. Intervention: Separate oral memantine, trazodone and shared placebo arms.

Primary endpoint: ALSFRS-R slope at interim assessment; survival also assessed.

Observed outcome: Neither arm slowed functional decline or improved survival.

Safety: Serious adverse events occurred in 20% on memantine, 14% on trazodone and 13% on placebo.

The two active ingredients were evaluated separately.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39307154/)

### PMID:20565333 · negative

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled single-center trial. Population: 63 people with probable or definite ALS. Intervention: Oral memantine 20 mg/day.

Primary endpoint: 12-month ALSFRS decline.

Observed outcome: No difference in primary or secondary measures.

Safety: Adverse events and laboratory changes were comparable to placebo.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20565333/)

### PMID:20839903 · biomarker

Open-label add-on pilot. Population: 20 people with sporadic ALS on riluzole. Intervention: Oral memantine 10 mg twice daily plus riluzole.

Primary endpoint: Safety and CSF biomarkers; function tracked descriptively.

Observed outcome: CSF tau fell in the treated cohort. No placebo-controlled slowing of decline was demonstrated.

Safety: Reported as well tolerated.

Biomarker-response subgroups were exploratory.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20839903/)
