# Riluzole

**Disposition: established treatment; comparator only.**

Human evidence category: favorable clinical evidence.

Survival favored riluzole. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8302340/) Adjusted survival analyses favored riluzole at 100 mg/day. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8676624/)

## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. Established comparator and current therapy. A human survival benefit does not identify incremental benefit from a delivery change. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8302340/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8676624/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:8302340 · favorable

Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial. Population: 155 people with ALS. Intervention: Oral riluzole.

Primary endpoint: Survival and rate of functional decline.

Observed outcome: Survival favored riluzole.

Safety: Asthenia, spasticity and aminotransferase elevations were reported.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8302340/)

### PMID:16193258 · safety

Small randomized add-on pilot. Population: Twenty ALS patients. Intervention: Oral minocycline 100 mg/day plus riluzole versus riluzole alone.

Primary endpoint: Combination safety; serial function and respiratory assessment.

Observed outcome: No significant adverse effects were detected in this pilot.

Safety: Twenty patients and six months of follow-up give limited power for safety.

The subsequent larger phase III minocycline trial documented functional harm.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16193258/)

### PMID:8676624 · favorable

Double-blind randomized dose-ranging trial. Population: 959 with probable or definite ALS. Intervention: Oral riluzole 50, 100 or 200 mg/day versus placebo.

Primary endpoint: Tracheostomy-free survival.

Observed outcome: Adjusted survival analyses favored riluzole at 100 mg/day.

Safety: Adverse effects were most frequent at the higher dose.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8676624/)
