## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. Oral dose escalation raised CSF drug levels. The phase III trial left motor-neuron HSP engagement uncertain. No measurement establishes that insufficient exposure caused its negative result. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18551622/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782015/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:38782015 · negative

Multicenter double-blind placebo-controlled phase III. Population: Early ALS, 239 in the modified intention-to-treat analysis. Intervention: Oral arimoclomol citrate, 400 mg three times daily.

Primary endpoint: Combined Assessment of Function and Survival at 76 weeks.

Observed outcome: No improvement in the primary function/survival endpoint.

Safety: Treatment-related events were more common. Adverse events led to discontinuation in 16% versus 5%.

Clinical target engagement was insufficiently characterized. A higher systemic dose raised tolerability concerns.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782015/)

### PMID:18551622 · pharmacokinetic

Randomized dose-ranging safety/PK trial. Population: 84 people with ALS; 44 in the PK subset. Intervention: Oral 25, 50 or 100 mg three times daily.

Primary endpoint: Safety and tolerability.

Observed outcome: CSF concentrations rose with oral dose; exposure in the target motor neurons was not established.

Safety: Up to 300 mg/day was tolerated in this short study.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18551622/)

### PMID:29367439 · safety

Blinded randomized placebo-controlled pilot. Population: 38 with rapidly progressive SOD1 ALS randomized. Intervention: Oral arimoclomol 200 mg three times daily.

Primary endpoint: Safety and tolerability.

Observed outcome: The pilot did not establish efficacy. Secondary survival estimate HR 0.77 (95% CI 0.32–1.80).

Safety: Tolerability was acceptable in this small cohort.

The secondary functional and survival estimates favored treatment imprecisely. Alex has no documented SOD1 result.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29367439/)

### NCT03836716 · safety

Single-arm extension of ORARIALS-01. Population: 120 ALS participants. Intervention: Oral arimoclomol.

Primary endpoint: Treatment-emergent events and laboratory safety.

Observed outcome: Open-label extension supplies longer-term safety observations without a concurrent efficacy control.

Safety: 21 of 120 had serious adverse events. Attribution cannot be inferred from the count.


[clinicaltrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03836716)
