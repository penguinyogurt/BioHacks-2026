## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. The riluzole add-on trial stopped for futility; biomarker findings with concomitant tretinoin do not establish a deficient motor-neuron exposure to pioglitazone. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22715372/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22830016/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:22715372 · negative

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial. Population: People with ALS on riluzole. Intervention: Oral pioglitazone 45 mg/day added to riluzole.

Primary endpoint: Survival.

Observed outcome: Stopped for futility. No survival improvement.

Safety: Described as well tolerated. The trial stopped for futility.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22715372/)

### PMID:22830016 · combination

Randomized placebo-controlled combination pilot. Population: ALS participants on riluzole. Intervention: Oral pioglitazone plus tretinoin.

Primary endpoint: ALSFRS-R progression and CSF biomarkers.

Observed outcome: No significant slowing of progression. CSF changes cannot be assigned to either ingredient alone.

Safety: Limited combination data do not establish individual or local-route safety.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22830016/)
