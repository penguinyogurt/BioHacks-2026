## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. Randomized treatment worsened arm strength. A higher local concentration has no established rationale for reversing that harm. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12939417/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:12939417 · harm

Multicenter double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial. Population: 296 people with ALS. Intervention: Oral topiramate, up to 800 mg/day.

Primary endpoint: Rate of loss of maximal isometric arm strength.

Observed outcome: Arm strength declined faster on topiramate (p=0.012).

Safety: Weight loss, anorexia and other adverse events were more frequent. Both weakness and weight loss are relevant to Alex.
