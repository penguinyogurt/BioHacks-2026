## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. Dose escalation changed plasma creatine and brain MRS measures. A clinically effective tissue concentration in ALS and an exposure deficit in the negative trials are undemonstrated. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20698808/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12666111/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:12666111 · negative

Double-blind randomized sequential trial. Population: 175 people with ALS. Intervention: Oral creatine monohydrate 10 g/day.

Primary endpoint: Death, persistent assisted ventilation or tracheostomy.

Observed outcome: No survival or functional benefit.

Safety: No important adverse reactions reported.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12666111/)

### PMID:18608093 · combination

Randomized active-combination selection design. Population: ALS participants in a phase II selection trial. Intervention: Oral celecoxib/creatine versus minocycline/creatine.

Primary endpoint: Six-month ALSFRS-R decline used to select a combination.

Observed outcome: Celecoxib/creatine was selected over minocycline/creatine; effects of individual ingredients could not be isolated.

Safety: Rash was more frequent with celecoxib/creatine.

A historical comparison did not establish placebo-controlled benefit.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18608093/)

### PMID:15534251 · negative

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial. Population: 104 ALS patients. Intervention: Oral creatine 5 g/day.

Primary endpoint: Maximal voluntary isometric contraction of eight arm muscles.

Observed outcome: No measured strength or functional benefit over six months.

Safety: Creatine was well tolerated.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15534251/)

### PMID:18608103 · negative

Multicenter double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial. Population: 107 with probable or definite ALS. Intervention: Oral creatine monohydrate 5 g/day.

Primary endpoint: Quantitative strength; respiratory function and ALSFRS also assessed.

Observed outcome: Motor, respiratory and functional measures did not significantly improve.

Safety: No safety estimate used for advancement.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18608103/)

### PMID:11677005 · exploratory

Uncontrolled supplementation cohort. Population: ALS patients assessed repeatedly. Intervention: Oral loading followed by maintenance creatine.

Primary endpoint: Isometric strength and fatigue.

Observed outcome: Short-term strength increases after loading were followed by progressive decline.

Safety: Comparative safety was not established.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11677005/)

### PMID:12061948 · inconclusive

Nonrandomized controlled study. Population: 14 treated advanced ALS patients and 13 controls. Intervention: Oral creatine 5 g/day.

Primary endpoint: Pulmonary function testing.

Observed outcome: Respiratory measures showed no consistent improvement; later dropout limited comparison.

Safety: Comparative adverse events were not quantified in the abstract.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12061948/)

### PMID:20698808 · pharmacokinetic

Phase I dose-escalation study. Population: Six ALS participants. Intervention: Escalating oral creatine monohydrate.

Primary endpoint: Plasma PK and brain spectroscopy.

Observed outcome: Plasma exposure rose with dose. Brain creatine MRS increased 8% (p=0.06).

Safety: No safety concerns reported in six participants.

Spectroscopy provides human exposure context but no evidence of clinical benefit.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20698808/)

### PMID:31608711 · exploratory

Randomized active-comparator selection trial. Population: ALS participants assigned high-dose creatine or tamoxifen regimens. Intervention: High-dose oral creatine arm.

Primary endpoint: Selection by ALSFRS-R decline.

Observed outcome: Higher-dose tamoxifen ranked numerically ahead of creatine. No placebo group to establish efficacy.

Safety: Drug-related adverse events and discontinuations were more frequent with high-dose creatine.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31608711/)
