## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. MND-SMART found no functional benefit from oral treatment. It did not establish low target-site exposure as the explanation. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39307154/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:39307154 · negative

Double-blind randomized MND-SMART platform, stage-two analysis. Population: Motor neuron disease including ALS. Intervention: Separate oral memantine, trazodone and shared placebo arms.

Primary endpoint: ALSFRS-R slope at interim assessment; survival also assessed.

Observed outcome: Neither arm slowed functional decline or improved survival.

Safety: Serious adverse events occurred in 20% on memantine, 14% on trazodone and 13% on placebo.

The two active ingredients were evaluated separately.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39307154/)
