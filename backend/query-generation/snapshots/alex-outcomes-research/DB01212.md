## Why would changing delivery change the result?

**Original delivery**. Both IV doses exceeded the prespecified in-vitro-derived CSF threshold. Motor-neuron engagement remained uncertain and serious hepatobiliary toxicity was observed. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24771634/) [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25297012/)

**Proposed focused ultrasound**. No reviewed drug-specific study showed that motor-cortex sonication corrects the relevant exposure or engagement limitation for this clinical outcome. The four-person ALS feasibility study measured temporary gadolinium enhancement. [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31558719/)

## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:25297012 · harm

Multistage double-blind randomized phase III. Population: People with ALS. Intervention: IV ceftriaxone, stage-three dose 4 g/day.

Primary endpoint: Functional decline and survival.

Observed outcome: No phase III efficacy and a material excess of serious hepatobiliary events at the tested regimen.

Safety: Serious hepatobiliary events in 41 treated participants (12%) versus none on placebo.

Earlier stages suggested a slower ALSFRS-R decline. The confirmatory stage did not reproduce it.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25297012/)

### PMID:23613806 · pharmacokinetic

Early stages of the later multistage randomized trial. Population: ALS participants in stages one and two. Intervention: IV ceftriaxone 2 or 4 g/day.

Primary endpoint: CSF exposure and tolerability for dose selection.

Observed outcome: Both doses exceeded the prespecified CSF trough threshold; no independent replicate of stage-three efficacy.

Safety: Biliary events were more frequent with ceftriaxone.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23613806/)

### PMID:24771634 · pharmacokinetic

Human plasma/CSF PK analysis of the same trial. Population: 66 ALS participants. Intervention: IV ceftriaxone.

Primary endpoint: CSF concentration relative to an in-vitro-derived threshold.

Observed outcome: Both regimens maintained CSF levels above the modeled threshold. Human tissue EAAT2 engagement remained uncertain.

Safety: No separate controlled safety comparison in this analysis.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24771634/)
