## Human studies and competing findings

### PMID:17014688 · biomarker

Uncontrolled human safety study with separate cell and mouse experiments. Population: 31 people with sporadic ALS. Intervention: Rectal melatonin 300 mg/day.

Primary endpoint: Safety and circulating oxidative damage; no controlled clinical efficacy endpoint.

Observed outcome: A serum oxidative-stress marker fell. Human functional or survival benefit remains unresolved.

Safety: The authors reported tolerability during follow-up.

Survival extension was observed in SOD1 mice. The human measurement was serum protein carbonyls.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17014688/)

### PMID:12220335 · safety

Early oral tolerability report. Population: ALS patients; indexed primary record has no abstract. Intervention: Daily oral melatonin.

Primary endpoint: Tolerability; clinical efficacy endpoint unavailable.

Observed outcome: Oral tolerability was reported. No usable comparative efficacy estimate was available.

Safety: Limited primary safety detail in the indexed report.


[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12220335/)

### PMID:33428242 · observational

Retrospective PRO-ACT database analysis. Population: ALS participants with reported melatonin use and comparison nonusers. Intervention: Self-reported exposure; dosing and route not standardized.

Primary endpoint: Association with time to death.

Observed outcome: An association with longer survival and slower functional decline is vulnerable to confounding and selection bias.

Safety: Safety was not the primary question.

Exposure was not assigned in this analysis.
[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33428242/)

### NCT06429059 · inconclusive

Open-label DIGAP-stratified pilot with lead-in; no matched placebo group for melatonin. Population: 14 assigned to the impaired autophagy/axonal transport stratum. Intervention: Melatonin; formulation not specified in registry intervention description.

Primary endpoint: ALSFRS-R from baseline through month nine.

Observed outcome: Mean ALSFRS-R was 34.4 at month three before treatment (14 measured) and 30.8 at month nine (13 measured). The design cannot establish a causal treatment effect.

Safety: One of 14 in the melatonin stratum had a serious adverse event. Causality unknown.

This clinical outcome is distinct from serum markers and from the PRO-ACT observational association.
[clinicaltrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06429059)
