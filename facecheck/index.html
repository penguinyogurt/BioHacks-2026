FaceCheck

Record a baseline of five facial expressions, then re-run the same protocol later and compare blendshape amplitude against your own baseline. A standalone test of whether MediaPipe-based facial tracking is sensitive enough to be a validation layer for the slurred-speech check.

Take your time getting into each expression — nothing advances on a timer, you click when you're ready. Go as big and exaggerated as you can for each one; a subtle expression won't read as different from a weak one.
